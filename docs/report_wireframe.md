# Report Wireframe

## Page 1: League Overview

Purpose: Give the audience the headline story quickly.

Recommended visuals:

- KPI cards: Total Salary, Total Points, Total Win Shares, Value Score
- Bar chart: Top 10 teams by payroll
- Scatter plot: Salary vs Production Score by player
- Slicers: Conference, Team, Position Group, QualifiedForValueRank

## Page 2: Player Value Rankings

Purpose: Show who produced the most relative to salary.

Recommended visuals:

- Table: Player, Team, Position, Salary Millions, PPG, WS, Value Score, Value Gap
- Bar chart: Top 15 players by Value Score
- Bar chart: Bottom 15 qualified players by Cost Per Win Share
- Tooltip fields: PER, TS%, USG%, VORP

## Page 3: Team Payroll Efficiency

Purpose: Compare team spending efficiency.

Recommended visuals:

- Matrix: Team, Payroll, Player Count, Total Win Shares, Cost Per Win Share, Value Score
- Bar chart: Teams by Value Score
- Scatter plot: Payroll Millions vs Total Win Shares
- Filled map is optional, but a conference/division slicer is usually cleaner.

## Page 4: Position and Contract Outliers

Purpose: Tell the “who is underpaid or overpaid?” story.

Recommended visuals:

- Bar chart: Average salary by position group
- Bar chart: Average value score by position group
- Table: Biggest positive Value Gap
- Table: Biggest negative Value Gap
- Slicer: All-Star flag

## Design Notes

Use a sports-broadcast feel, but keep it readable:

- Dark navy or charcoal background
- White cards and table text
- Accent color in orange/gold or team-neutral blue
- Avoid using too many team colors at once
- Keep the player ranking table as the main analytical object
